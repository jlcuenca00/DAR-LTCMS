# Frequently Asked Questions

## Does an Approved clearance mean ownership was already transferred?

No. Approval records the administrative clearance decision. It does not automatically transfer ownership or modify registry records.

## Can a landowner submit an application through DAR-LTCMS?

No. Applications are manually encoded and processed by authorized DAR Staff.

## Why can I not edit an Approved or historical finalized application?

Approved is the only current final decision state. DAR-LTCMS locks substantive changes after approval to preserve integrity and traceability. Older Not Approved / Denied records remain historical and read-only.

## Is release the same as approval?

No. Approved is the only current final application decision. Release is a separate record showing whether the signed clearance output has been delivered to the client. Issues that block approval remain open through Compliance Required rather than becoming a new negative final decision.

## Can landowners see other landowners' parcels?

No. Landowner access is restricted to records linked to their own account.

## Does the system replace official DAR legal review?

No. System checks are assistive. Official decisions remain with authorized DAR personnel and established administrative procedures.

## Why does my new account show a pending page?

Landowner registration needs Staff review and the correct record link. Contact the office if access remains pending. Registration does not create an application.

## What should I do when an application says Compliance Required?

Read the Action Required instructions and provide the requested correction or material through the normal office process. Staff record the response and resolve the notice; the same application then resumes its saved stage.

## Where did the login events go?

They are available under **Login History** in Audit Logs. The default **Activity** view keeps record and workflow actions separate.
