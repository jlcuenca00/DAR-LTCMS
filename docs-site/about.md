# About DAR-LTCMS

DAR-LTCMS is a **web-based administrative processing and monitoring system** intended for the **Department of Agrarian Reform Negros Oriental Provincial Office**.

Its purpose is to improve the organization, traceability, monitoring, and generation of land transfer clearance records while maintaining clear role boundaries and audit trails.

The platform serves as a **decision-support and records-management tool**. Validation checks help authorized users review data and documentary requirements, but they do not replace official DAR legal or administrative judgment.

## Authorized user groups

### DAR Staff

DAR Staff manually encode and manage applications and related records, review supporting documents, process the office workflow, generate clearance outputs, and produce monitoring reports.

### Landowners

Landowners do not create clearance applications. Their access is limited to their own linked records and application status information.

### Geodetic Personnel

Geodetic personnel have limited access focused on parcel, coordinate, reference, and map-related verification. Their access does not grant broad authority to change ownership or application decisions.
