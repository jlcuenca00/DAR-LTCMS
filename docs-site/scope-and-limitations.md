# Scope & Limitations

## In scope

DAR-LTCMS covers:

- Landowner record management
- Parcel record management
- Landholding record management
- Land transfer clearance application processing
- Supporting document upload and review
- Application status monitoring
- Role-based access control
- Audit logging
- Monitoring and report generation
- Clearance generation and release tracking

## Important limitations

DAR-LTCMS is **not** an automatic land ownership transfer system and is **not** a registry mutation engine.

An **Approved** clearance means that the administrative clearance decision has been recorded and the corresponding clearance output may be generated and processed for release.

It does **not** mean that:

- land ownership has automatically changed;
- a parcel has automatically been transferred to another person;
- Registry of Deeds records have been modified; or
- the legal transfer has been conclusively finalized.

Any actual ownership transfer, registry alteration, or legal mutation is outside the automatic operational scope of DAR-LTCMS and remains subject to separate legal and administrative procedures.

## Geographic and organizational boundary

The system is designed for the **DAR Negros Oriental Provincial Office** and its authorized stakeholders.
