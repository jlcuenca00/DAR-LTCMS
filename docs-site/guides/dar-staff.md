# DAR Staff Guide

DAR Staff are the primary administrative users of DAR-LTCMS.

## Main responsibilities

DAR Staff can:

- create and maintain landowner records;
- manage parcel and landholding records;
- manually encode land transfer clearance applications;
- link the subject parcel to an application;
- upload and review supporting requirements;
- record payment and Official Receipt information;
- process applications through the authorized workflow;
- generate clearance outputs;
- record readiness for release and client release;
- view monitoring reports; and
- review audit logs according to authorization.

## Final decision rule

Once an application reaches **Approved** or **Denied**, substantive editing and document uploads are locked.

Release-related actions remain available only to authorized users and do not alter the final decision.

## Data integrity

Before moving an application forward, confirm that the linked parcel, parties, documentary requirements, and relevant area values are correct. Important actions are recorded for traceability.
