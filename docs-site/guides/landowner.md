# Landowner Guide

Landowner accounts provide limited access to information connected to the authenticated landowner.

## What landowners can do

Depending on the records linked by DAR Staff, a landowner may:

- sign in to the system;
- view their own linked parcel information;
- view their own application status; and
- access released clearance information when made available by the office.

## What landowners cannot do

Landowners cannot:

- create land transfer clearance applications;
- edit official parcel, landholding, or application records;
- access another landowner's records; or
- change an application decision.

If your account is not yet linked to the correct record, contact the DAR Negros Oriental Provincial Office for assistance.

## Registration and access

Public registration, including the configured Google option, is for Landowner accounts. A new account may remain pending until Staff review it and link the correct Landowner record. Registration does not create an application or give Staff permissions.

## Compliance Required

An unresolved compliance notice appears as **Action Required / Compliance Required** on your own linked application. Read the instructions and submit the requested material through the normal DAR office process. Staff record and review it in the system; the portal does not let you upload compliance documents. After Staff resolve the notice, the same application resumes its earlier processing stage.

## Final output

An Approved decision does not by itself make the final output available in the Landowner portal. Access follows actual **Released to Client** recording and the account's link to the application.
