# DAR-LTCMS Documentation

Welcome to the documentation for the **Department of Agrarian Reform Land Transfer Clearance and Monitoring System (DAR-LTCMS)** of the **DAR Negros Oriental Provincial Office**.

DAR-LTCMS is a web-based administrative processing, records-management, clearance-generation, and monitoring platform for authorized DAR personnel and stakeholders.

## What the system does

DAR-LTCMS supports:

- landowner, parcel, and landholding record management;
- manual encoding and processing of land transfer clearance applications by DAR Staff;
- upload and review of supporting documents;
- application status monitoring;
- limited parcel and map review for authorized geodetic personnel;
- role-based access control;
- audit logging;
- monitoring and report generation; and
- generation and recording of land transfer clearance results.

!!! important "Clearance approval is not an automatic land transfer"
    Approval of a clearance application does **not** automatically transfer land ownership, alter Registry of Deeds records, or legally mutate ownership. Any actual ownership transfer or registry alteration remains subject to separate legal and administrative procedures.
