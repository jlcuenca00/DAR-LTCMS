# Land Transfer Clearance Workflow

The workflow follows the administrative clearance process used by the DAR Negros Oriental Provincial Office.

1. **Application encoded and reviewed for completeness**  
   DAR Staff encode the application, link the subject parcel, and review the documentary requirements.

2. **Returned for compliance when incomplete**  
   Missing or deficient requirements are returned for compliance. This is not a final denial.

3. **Payment order and Official Receipt recording**  
   Once documentary intake is complete, the payment order is prepared. Payment is handled through the appropriate cashier process, and the Official Receipt is recorded in DAR-LTCMS.

4. **Endorsement to LTID**  
   The complete application is endorsed for land tenure-related verification.

5. **LTID verification and return to Legal**  
   Parcel and related verification information is reviewed and returned to Legal.

6. **Legal evaluation and CSW preparation**  
   Legal evaluation and the required case summary or supporting administrative documentation are prepared.

7. **Chief Legal review**

8. **Forwarding to PARPO II**

9. **PARPO II final decision**  
   The application becomes either **Approved** or **Denied**. These are final decision states in the system.

10. **Clearance output preparation and release tracking**  
    The signed clearance result can be marked ready for release and later recorded as released to the client.

!!! note
    Release tracking is separate from the final application decision. An application can remain **Approved** or **Denied** while its release status records whether the signed document has been delivered.

!!! important
    No workflow step automatically changes parcel ownership or registry records.
